Objective: To outline requirements for automating Sonic Group's expense and sales bill approval workflows and to explore
integration options with Tally Prime and Spine.

Key discussion points: Sonic Group handles commercial space rentals and fit-outs, currently managing expenses via a slow, manual,
hard-copy approval process. A mobile application is needed for location coordinators to photograph bills, eliminating manual
scanning and data entry. The application requires a 4-tier approval workflow: Center Coordinator -> Head Office Coordinator ->
Director -> Accounts. The system needs sorting capabilities (by date, vendor) and a feature to raise queries on incorrect bills.
AI is required to read bill images, extract data like GST and narrations, and map them to correct expense heads and specific Tally
ledger names (to be provided by Mugdha). Sales/income invoices need to be auto-generated in Tally one day before the due date based
on agreement data provided by Sonic Group. Direct API push into Tally may not be feasible; as a workaround, the application can
generate an XML file for Tally import. Sonic Group currently uses the Spine application.

Decisions: 4-stage approval workflow. If direct entry into Tally is technically unfeasible, the system will generate an XML file
for data import. Mugdha will provide the mapping document connecting vendor names on invoices to their respective ledger names and
expense heads in Tally. PuchoAI will develop a basic UI/UX mockup.

Mugdha Borate: we have a company called Sonic Group. Here, we take commercial spaces on rent and lease them out, which sometimes
includes fit-out work. Sometimes it is only fit-out and there is no rent. We want to automate our entire process. The Director
needs everything in one online access. We have multiple locations across different states, here in Maharashtra as well as in
other states. They have certain invoices at those locations, like expense bills. The person in charge there verifies the entire
bill and sends it to the coordinator here. Then the coordinator verifies it. The coordinator hands it over to the accounts team,
and then it goes to the Director. The Director approves it. We use Tally Prime. For our expenses, the coordinator at that
particular location will just take a photo from their mobile and upload it. The coordinator will approve it, then our Head Office
coordinator will also approve it. Then the Director will also check and approve it online, and then it will come to Accounts, and
whatever finalization is needed will be done in Accounts. I can see all the invoices in that application. If my Director asks to
see how many bills came on a particular day, I should be able to sort it by day. There should be some drop-down options. If it is
related to a particular vendor, I need to see how many bills have come from that vendor since we started, completely categorized
by vendor. If we have raised any queries, we will put them in the application. If a bill seems incorrect, we can flag it and add
remarks. Whatever correction or reply comes from them, it will come through that. AI should read this bill, create an XML or
Excel file out of it, and integrate it into Tally. We have an income part. We have agreements, and we will provide you with the
summaries of the agreements. Whatever my expected sales are from the agreement, that should also be visible to me in your
application. We have a due date, so exactly one day before that due date, its entry should be made and the invoice should be
generated in Tally. Data will come from the photos of the bills for the expenses. For the sales invoices we will provide you with
the data. It will be at 4 places: the center where it is received, then the HO coordinator, then the Director, and then
Accounts. It should happen with one click. If your application can create an XML file, then an import link is definitely
possible. Suppose the name on the invoice is XYZ Private Limited. If it is from Bangalore, in my Tally it might be XYZ Private
Limited Bangalore. Which vendor's invoice is registered under which name in my books here, I will provide that to you. The file
should come exactly according to the proper heads. You need to read the GST in it, you need to read its narration, you need to
read the GST rates, and those entries should come properly and fall under the correct head. You will also have to read exactly
which expense head it is, and the entry will go according to that.
